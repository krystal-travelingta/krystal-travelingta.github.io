My story

It started with a someday of my own.

Sunset on the dunes of Huacachina, Peru. The solo trip I waited years to take, and the reason Traveling the Atlas exists.

Built from a love of travel, not just a business plan.

  1. GROWING UP

    Where it began

    As a kid, vacation meant visiting family. Trips to my great-grandparents in Brownsville, Texas came with walks across the border into Mexico, and a California trip for my uncle's wedding gave me my first look at mountains, the Grand Canyon and the ocean. I never forgot that feeling.

  2. RAISING THREE KIDS

    Small trips, big memories

    As a single mom, time and money were tight. We went anyway. Oregon, California, Nevada, Oklahoma, Texas, Florida. Those simple trips became some of our family's very best memories, and they taught me that the right experience matters more than the price tag.

  3. FINDING ME AGAIN

    The world opened up

    Once my kids were grown, I started rediscovering who I was beyond being Mom. Car rallies with my partner Gary took us across the country and on to Panama, St. Lucia and Mexico. Somewhere along the way, I realized that helping friends plan their trips made me almost as happy as taking my own.

  4. PERU

    My someday, finally

    Peru had been on my list for years. I finally went, solo. I sandboarded down the dunes, pushed far past my comfort zone, and came home certain of one thing: I wanted to help other people stop waiting and take the trips they keep talking about.

  5. TODAY

    Traveling the Atlas

    Now I design extraordinary travel for couples who have worked hard to build their lives and are ready to enjoy them. I'm not really selling flights and hotels. I'm giving you time together, moments that stop you in your tracks, and stories you'll still be telling years from now.

You worked hard to build a life worth enjoying. Don't spend the best years of it waiting for someday.WHAT I BELIEVE
