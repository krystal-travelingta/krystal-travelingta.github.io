The Trip

India's most famous train lives up to its name, with royal-inspired cabins, a lounge bar and every meal and drink included. Over seven nights it loops through Rajasthan's palace cities, from pink Jaipur to golden Jaisalmer and blue Jodhpur, with a tiger reserve and the Taj Mahal along the way. Delhi bookends the trip, so you have time to explore before you board and to unwind after.

Krystal Winchel, Founder, Traveling the Atlas

Day by Day

Your itinerary

Day01
Delhi

Welcome to India

You're met on arrival and taken to your hotel for the first two nights. India's capital is a fascinating mix of ancient history and modern energy. Spend the rest of the day at your own pace, perhaps browsing a nearby market or enjoying your first real taste of Indian cooking.

Meals: On your ownStay options: The Leela Palace · The Imperial · JW Marriott Hotel New Delhi Aerocity · The LaLit · Pullman Aerocity · Pride Plaza Aerocity
Day02
Delhi

Old & New Delhi

A full day with your private guide shows you both sides of the city. Wind through the busy lanes and markets of Old Delhi, then contrast them with the grand monuments and wide, tree-lined avenues of New Delhi. It's the best possible introduction to the history and culture you'll see unfold all week.

Meals: BreakfastStay options: The Leela Palace · The Imperial · JW Marriott Hotel New Delhi Aerocity · The LaLit · Pullman Aerocity · Pride Plaza Aerocity
Day03
Delhi

All Aboard the Palace on Wheels

Enjoy a relaxed morning before your transfer to the station. A traditional welcome awaits as you board, and you'll be shown to your cabin and the train's lounges. As Delhi slips away behind you, settle into the royal surroundings. Rajasthan is waiting.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, DinnerStay: Your cabin aboard the Palace on Wheels
Day04
Jaipur

The Pink City

Wake up in Jaipur, Rajasthan's capital and a city built in shades of rose and terracotta. The day takes you to the hilltop Amber Fort, the City Palace and the remarkable Jantar Mantar observatory, with time for the colorful bazaars in between.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, DinnerStay: Your cabin aboard the Palace on Wheels
Day05
Ranthambore & Chittorgarh

Tigers & a Legendary Fort

Begin in Ranthambore, one of India's most celebrated wildlife reserves, where tigers roam among old ruins and lakes. Later, continue to Chittorgarh, whose vast hilltop fort is woven with stories of courage and sacrifice. Wildlife in the morning and royal history by afternoon make this a standout day.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, DinnerStay: Your cabin aboard the Palace on Wheels
Day06
Udaipur

The City of Lakes

Udaipur might be the most romantic city in India. Explore the sprawling City Palace, then take a boat out on Lake Pichola for views of palaces that seem to float on the water. It's a peaceful change of pace in the middle of the week.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, DinnerStay: Your cabin aboard the Palace on Wheels
Day07
Jaisalmer

The Golden City

Deep in the Thar Desert, Jaisalmer rises from the sand in honey-colored stone. Explore the living fort, where people still make their homes inside the walls, and the intricately carved Patwon ki Haveli. As the light turns gold, it's easy to see where the nickname comes from.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, DinnerStay: Your cabin aboard the Palace on Wheels
Day08
Jodhpur

The Blue City

Jodhpur is crowned by Mehrangarh, one of the most impressive forts in India, towering over a sea of blue-washed houses. Explore the fort and the white marble memorial of Jaswant Thada, with time to soak up the old town before returning to the train.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, DinnerStay: Your cabin aboard the Palace on Wheels
Day09
Bharatpur & Agra

Birdlife & the Taj Mahal

Start at Bharatpur's renowned bird sanctuary, a wetland haven for resident and migrating species. Then it's on to Agra for the moment you've been waiting for, the Taj Mahal, along with the red sandstone walls of Agra Fort and the Mughal grandeur that surrounds them.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, DinnerStay: Your cabin aboard the Palace on Wheels
Day10
Delhi

Back to Delhi

Step off the train this morning and transfer to your hotel for a final night. The rest of the day is yours to revisit a favorite spot, do some last-minute shopping, or simply relax and take in everything you've seen.

Meals: On your ownStay options: The Leela Palace · The Imperial · JW Marriott Hotel New Delhi Aerocity · The LaLit · Pullman Aerocity · Pride Plaza Aerocity
Day11
Delhi

Homeward

Check out after breakfast and continue your travels. If you'd like more time in India, ask me about adding Varanasi, the Himalayan foothills or the backwaters of Kerala.

Meals: Breakfast

Krystal's Tip

When to go

The Palace on Wheels runs seasonally, generally from autumn through spring, and the cooler months from November to February are the most comfortable for sightseeing in Rajasthan. The train departs on set dates, so I'll build your Delhi nights around the departure you choose. Pack layers, since desert mornings can be surprisingly cool.
